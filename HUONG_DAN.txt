Dat clear_data.js canh package.json cua DevPulse. Can Node.js 24+.

Dung app truoc khi xoa:
systemctl --user stop devpulse.service

Xem truoc (khong xoa):
node clear_data.js --dry-run

Xoa toan bo ban ghi trong database:
node clear_data.js

Bat lai app:
systemctl --user start devpulse.service

Giu nguyen schema, .env va settings.json. Xoa ca lich su chi phi va lich chay. Scheduler co the lay tin va goi AI lai khi bat app.
